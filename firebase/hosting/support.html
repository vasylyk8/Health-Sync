Common questions

Claude or ChatGPT says it can't reach KROK. For a public OAuth connection, sign in with Apple inside KROK first, then authorize the assistant using the same Apple Account. If authorization has expired or you disconnected, reconnect in the assistant. See connection instructions. For an existing private-link connection, check the assistant's setup in KROK and reconnect if the link was revoked.

My latest workout is missing. Open the KROK app once and pull down to sync. New workouts are sent shortly after they end, but iOS pauses background syncing if you swipe the app away. The workout summary arrives first; its detailed data (heart rate, route) follows and the assistant will tell you if it is still uploading.

How do I delete my data? In KROK, tap ••• → Delete All My Data. Everything on our servers is erased within 24 hours.
