Common questions

Claude or ChatGPT says it can't reach KROK. Open the KROK app, tap the assistant, and check it shows "Set up". If you removed the connector in Claude or ChatGPT, tap Disconnect in KROK and connect again to get a new link.

My data looks out of date. Open the KROK app once; it syncs immediately. iOS pauses background syncing if you swipe the app away.

How do I delete my data? In KROK, tap ••• → Delete All My Data. Everything on our servers is erased within 24 hours.
