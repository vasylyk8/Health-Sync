New to KROK? Start on your iPhone.

KROK requires its iPhone app to sync the Apple Health data you choose.

  1. Install KROK on your iPhone.
  2. Open KROK, connect Apple Health, sign in with Apple, and let your data sync.
  3. Return to your assistant and connect KROK using the same Apple Account.
