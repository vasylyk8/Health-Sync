KROK

Connect KROK to your assistant

  1. Install KROK on your iPhone, connect Apple Health, and let your workouts sync.
  2. In KROK, sign in with Apple to link your synced data to your account.
  3. Add KROK in your assistant and authorize it using the same Apple Account.

Until KROK is listed in the directories, add a custom connector using https://krok-1d60a.firebaseapp.com/mcp and OAuth authentication. ChatGPT custom connections require developer mode on an eligible plan.

Try these questions

  • List my workouts this week.
  • What were the kilometre splits in my latest run?
  • How did I sleep this week?

Permissions and privacy

KROK provides read-only workout measurements and daily summaries. GPS routes hide the first and last 300 metres unless you grant full-route access and explicitly request the exact locations.

Troubleshooting

If your data is missing, sign in with Apple inside KROK first, using the same account as browser login. Open the app and pull down to sync. Incomplete data is labelled in tool results.

Authorization lasts up to 30 days. If your assistant asks you to reconnect, repeat authorization. Disconnect an assistant in KROK to revoke all of its current grants. Delete All My Data stops access immediately and removes the server copy; Apple Health is unchanged.
