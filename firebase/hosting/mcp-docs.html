Connect KROK to your assistant

KROK requires its iPhone app to sync your chosen Apple Health data before an assistant can read it.

  1. Install KROK on your iPhone. Connect Apple Health and let your workouts sync.

  2. Sign in with Apple in the app. This links your synced data to your account.
  3. Add KROK in your assistant. Start the connection in Claude or ChatGPT and authorize it using the same Apple Account.

Add a custom connector

Until KROK is listed in the directories, add a custom connector in your assistant with OAuth authentication and this server URL:

https://krok-1d60a.firebaseapp.com/mcp

ChatGPT custom connections require developer mode on an eligible plan.

Try these questions

  • List my workouts this week.
  • What were the kilometre splits in my latest run?
  • How did I sleep this week?

Permissions and privacy

KROK provides read-only workout measurements and daily summaries. GPS routes hide the first and last 300 metres unless you grant full-route access and explicitly request the exact locations.

Troubleshooting

My data is missing

Sign in with Apple inside KROK first, using the same account as browser login. Open the app and pull down to sync. Incomplete data is labelled in tool results.

My assistant asks me to reconnect

Authorization lasts up to 30 days. Repeat authorization when asked.

Disconnecting or deleting data

Disconnect an assistant in KROK to revoke all of its current grants. Delete All My Data stops access immediately and removes the server copy; Apple Health is unchanged.
