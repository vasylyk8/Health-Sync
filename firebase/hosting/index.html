Your Apple Health, meet your AI.

Ask Claude or ChatGPT about your runs, pace, sleep and recovery, using the Apple Health data you choose to share.

New to KROK?

Install the iPhone app first. It syncs your workouts so your assistant can read them.

Already set up?

Open Claude or ChatGPT, add KROK, and sign in with the Apple Account you use in the app.

Connect your assistant

Ask things like

  • “What were my kilometre splits in yesterday’s run?”
  • “How did I sleep this week?”
  • “Is my heart rate drifting on long runs?”
  • “Am I on track for my marathon goal?”

How it works

  1. Install and sync. Connect Apple Health in the KROK iPhone app.
  2. Sign in with Apple. This links your synced data to your account.
  3. Ask your assistant. Authorize KROK in Claude or ChatGPT and start asking.

Your data, your choice.

  • Read-only access, authorized separately for each assistant.
  • Routes hide the first and last 300 metres unless you give separate consent.
  • Disconnect an assistant or delete your server data in KROK at any time.

KROK describes your data and trends; it does not provide medical advice.
