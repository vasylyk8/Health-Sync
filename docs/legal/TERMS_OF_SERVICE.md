# KROK terms of service

Effective date: October 2, 2026.

## Service and eligibility
KROK is provided by 2ndOp Inc. It copies Apple Health data you permit to its servers and gives read-only access to assistants you explicitly connect. KROK is intended for people aged 16 or older. The intended launch availability is all supported countries except Russia and Belarus, subject to the availability of Apple, Firebase and the assistant platform.

## Your choices and responsibilities
Use KROK only with your own account and data. Choose which optional data groups to sync and which assistants to connect. Protect private links and account credentials. Do not use KROK to access another person's data or interfere with the service. You may disconnect assistants and delete your server data from the app at any time.

## Health information
KROK is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or provide medical advice. Its outputs describe data and trends; data may be incomplete or delayed. Seek a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions and emergency services for an emergency. Your connected assistant operates under its own terms and may retain information it already received.
